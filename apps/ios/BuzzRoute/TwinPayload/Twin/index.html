What you are looking at

Buildings, roads and green space come from OpenStreetMap. Buses replay real GPS polls from the Stinger Delay project (every 15 s, March 2026), map-matched onto the official route polylines. Blue has no logs, so its buses are simulated (translucent). The sun and shadows follow pvlib solar geometry for the chosen date and time.
